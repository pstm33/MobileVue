TAGAM DELIVERY

Account and data deletion

Tagam Delivery users can request deletion of their account and related personal data directly from the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Tagam Delivery.
  2. Go to Profile, then Account security.
  3. Open Account deletion and follow the confirmation flow.

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to support@tagam.delivery and include the phone number or email used for your account.

Data deleted or retained

We delete account profile data, saved addresses, saved payment references and app account identifiers when deletion is completed. Some order, payment and tax records may be retained when required for fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution or legal compliance.
